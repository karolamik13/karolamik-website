Research

We combine and adapt computational approaches to address mechanistic questions across medically and biotechnologically relevant molecular processes.

01

Regulated Cell Death & Redox Biology

We investigate molecular mechanisms that control regulated cell death, with a major focus on ferroptosis, oxidative lipid signaling and the enzymatic generation and removal of lipid death signals.

Current themes include the lipoxygenase/PEBP1 system, lipoxygenase-family mechanisms, phospholipases such as iPLA2β, oxygen access to catalytic sites, redox-dependent molecular recognition and mechanisms of ferroptosis regulation and inhibition.

02

Lipid–Protein & Membrane Interactions

We study how proteins recognize, bind and transform membrane lipids, phospholipid substrates and lipid mediators, and how the membrane environment reshapes protein function and selectivity.

Systems range from lipoxygenases and oxidized phospholipids to cardiolipin-dependent protein interactions, with emphasis on membrane association, lipid specificity, lipid-mediated regulation and molecular recognition.

03

Biotechnological Enzymes & Catalytic Mechanisms

We use molecular modeling to resolve catalytic mechanisms, substrate recognition, allosteric communication, electron transfer and substrate channeling in enzymes of biological and biotechnological interest.

Published and ongoing systems include the AlkB–AlkG electron-transfer complex, SPase and other enzymes in which dynamics, molecular interactions and catalytic architecture determine activity and specificity.

Explore this research area →
04

Computational Methods & Software Development

We develop computational methods that turn structural and dynamical information into interpretable molecular mechanisms, with a strong focus on reusable tools for the biomolecular modeling community.

Our work contributes to ProDy and includes methods for interactions, hydration, channels, tunnels, pores, surface cavities, mechanical stiffness, protein dynamics and comparative analysis of protein families.
