Software

Tools for biomolecular structure, dynamics, interactions, hydration, internal pathways and mechanics.

CaviTracer

CaviTracer identifies and characterizes channels, pores and surface-connected cavities in protein structures, structural ensembles and molecular dynamics trajectories. It enables geometric and residue-level analysis of internal pathways and cavities relevant to ligand transport, catalysis and molecular recognition. Comparative analyses across homologous proteins can reveal conserved and variable channel, pore and cavity features within protein families.

InSty

InSty is a ProDy module for analyzing seven interaction types: hydrogen bonds, salt bridges, repulsive ionic interactions, π–π stacking, cation–π interactions, hydrophobic interactions and disulfide bonds. It can be applied to single and multi-model PDB structures, structural ensembles and MD trajectories to assess interaction patterns and their persistence. Comparative analysis of homologous proteins enables identification of conserved and variable interactions across protein families.

WatFinder

WatFinder is a ProDy tool for identifying and analyzing protein–water interactions, including water bridges, water-mediated associations, water channels and water clusters. It can be applied to individual structures, structural ensembles, multi-model PDB files, molecular dynamics trajectories and homologous proteins to identify recurrent and conserved hydration patterns. Analysis of water clusters and preferred hydration sites can also help reveal regions relevant to ligand binding and potential small-molecule binding pockets.

Mechanical stiffness calculations

MechStiff

MechStiff is a ProDy tool for evaluating the mechanical stiffness of protein structures using elastic network models. It enables the identification of mechanically resistant and flexible regions and the analysis of how structural elements respond to deformation. The method can help characterize protein mechanics and relate local mechanical properties to structural organization and function.
