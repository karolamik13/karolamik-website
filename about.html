Karolina Mikulska-Ruminska is a professor in the Department of Biophysics, Institute of Physics, Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń. Her research combines computational biophysics, structural modeling and redox biology to investigate how molecular interactions, dynamics and catalytic events shape biomolecular function.

Her scientific path developed from medical physics, single-molecule biophysics and protein nanomechanics toward protein dynamics, redox lipid biology and ferroptosis. Alongside biological applications, she develops computational approaches for studying protein interactions, hydration, internal pathways, cavities and mechanical properties within the ProDy framework.

She completed postdoctoral research at the University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine and has held visiting appointments at Stony Brook University, the University of Pittsburgh and Jiangnan University.
