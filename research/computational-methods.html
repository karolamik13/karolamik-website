Computational Methods & Software Development

Overview

We develop computational methods that turn structural and dynamical information into interpretable molecular mechanisms, with a strong focus on reusable tools for the biomolecular modeling community.

Current themes

  • Protein interactions and structural stability
  • Hydration and water-mediated interactions
  • Channels, tunnels, pores and surface cavities
  • Mechanical stiffness and protein dynamics
  • Comparative analysis across homologous protein families
  • Method and software development within the ProDy framework
