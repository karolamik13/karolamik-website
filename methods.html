Methods

We combine standard computational approaches with dedicated strategies for challenging systems, including metalloproteins, metalloenzymes, membrane-associated complexes, cofactors and non-standard molecular species.

Molecular Dynamics

Atomistic simulations of conformational dynamics, interactions and mechanistic transitions.

Molecular Docking

Protein–ligand, lipid–protein and substrate-recognition problems with mechanistic interpretation.

Metalloprotein Modeling

Metal-containing proteins and metalloenzymes, including non-heme metal centers and heme-containing systems.

Non-standard Parameterization

Dedicated treatment of unusual ligands, cofactors, catalytic centers and chemically modified molecular species.

Membrane & Lipid Systems

Protein–membrane complexes, phospholipid substrates, oxidized lipids and lipid mediators.

Protein Dynamics & Allostery

Elastic network models, collective motions and dynamic communication pathways.

Structural Bioinformatics

Interfaces, hydration, channels, tunnels, pores, cavities and comparative structural analysis.

Quantum-Chemical Approaches

Targeted electronic-structure calculations supporting mechanistic interpretation and parameter development.
